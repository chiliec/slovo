Russian learning app — visual direction exploration
Working name SLOVO ("word") — rename anytime. Assumptions: Gen-Z / young adults, heavy gamification (streak, XP, league, mascot), UI in English with Russian only as learning content. Round 1 shows one screen — the home / lesson hub, the identity-defining screen — in 4 directions with distinct layout, type, palette and mascot. Two on iOS, two on Android; the winning direction gets built for both. Next round: pick a direction (or a mix) and I'll expand it into the lesson exercise flow + celebration moments as an interactive prototype.
