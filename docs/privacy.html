SLOVO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

SLOVO (“the app”) is a Russian words-and-phrases learning app. Your privacy is simple to explain because the app is built to need nothing from you.

What we collect

Nothing. SLOVO does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no account and no sign-up.
  • The app makes no network requests. All learning content and audio is bundled inside the app and runs fully offline.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress — XP, streaks, which lessons you have completed, and spaced-repetition review state — is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Children’s privacy

SLOVO does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Audio attribution

Audio recordings come from the Tatoeba project and are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY). Per-clip attribution is listed inside the app under YOU → About → View Full Credits.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page.
