SLOVO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

SLOVO (“the app”) is a Russian words-and-phrases learning app. Your privacy is simple to explain because the app is built to need nothing from you.

What we collect

SLOVO does not collect personal data.

  • The app has no account and no sign-up.
  • All learning content and audio is bundled inside the app and works offline.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. The app sends which screen you opened (for example "learn" or "review") to our self-hosted Umami instance at analytics.nextgensoft.co, together with the app version and your device model / OS version. Umami is cookieless and stores no persistent identifier: your IP address is used only to derive an approximate (country/city) location and a short-lived session hash, and is not stored. This data is never shared with third parties and is used only to see which parts of the app are used.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress — XP, streaks, which lessons you have completed, and spaced-repetition review state — is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Children’s privacy

SLOVO does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Audio attribution

Audio recordings come from the Tatoeba project and are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY). Per-clip attribution is listed inside the app under YOU → About → View Full Credits.
