06 · SOUND & HAPTICS
Toy synth in space — rounded sine + marimba, pitched playful, mixed quiet. Rewards rise in pitch; mistakes soften (never punish). Lesson complete = 4-note liftoff arpeggio + one Kosmo "boop" (1.2s).
