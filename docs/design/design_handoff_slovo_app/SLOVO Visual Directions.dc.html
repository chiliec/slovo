8aSOUND DESIGN — toy synth in space
SONIC IDENTITY
Toy synth in space: rounded sine + marimba, pitched playful, mixed quiet. Rewards rise in pitch, mistakes soften — audio never punishes. Every sound pairs with a matching haptic.

Two-note marimba rise (C5→E5), warm and short
250ms
ANSWER WRONG
Soft felt "womp", pitch down — never a buzzer
300ms

Click with a tiny sparkle tail; combo pops rise with count

4-note liftoff arpeggio + one Kosmo "boop" bark

Reverse-cymbal whoosh resolving into a warm chord

Single Sputnik "bip" — the brand in one blip
150ms
