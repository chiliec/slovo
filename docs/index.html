SLOVO

Useful Russian words and phrases, with real human audio.

SLOVO is a gamified phrasebook trainer for people who enjoy Russian and want to pick up genuinely useful language — no grammar drills, no pressure. It works entirely offline: no account, no network, no ads, no tracking.

How it works

  • Work through 8 themed units and 24 bite-sized lessons — greetings, cafés, getting around, shopping, time, small talk and more.
  • Study a lesson's phrases, then prove it with recognition and recall quizzes.
  • Optional transliteration shows Latin spellings while you get comfortable with Cyrillic — switch it off when you no longer need it.
  • Earn XP, keep a daily streak, and watch each unit's mastery climb.
  • Spaced repetition brings phrases back just before you would forget them.
