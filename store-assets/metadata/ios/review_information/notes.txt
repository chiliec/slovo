SLOVO is an offline Russian words-and-phrases trainer. No account, login, or
demo credentials are needed — launch the app and tap START on the first lesson.
All content, audio, and progress are on-device. The only network traffic is
anonymous screen-view analytics (screen name, app/OS version, device model; no
identifiers) sent to our self-hosted Umami at analytics.nextgensoft.co, as
declared in App Privacy and the privacy manifest.

Third-party content: every audio clip is a human recording from the Tatoeba
project (https://tatoeba.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
(CC-BY). Per-clip attribution ships inside the app and is reachable in two taps:
the YOU tab -> About -> VIEW FULL CREDITS.
