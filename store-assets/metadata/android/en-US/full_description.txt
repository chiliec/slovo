SLOVO is a gamified phrasebook trainer for people who enjoy Russian and want to pick up genuinely useful words and phrases — no grammar drills, no pressure.

Meet Misha, your bear guide, and work through bite-sized lessons grouped into themed units: greetings, cafe & food, getting around, shopping, time, small talk and more. Every phrase comes with a real human audio recording, so you learn how Russian actually sounds.

WHY SLOVO
• Real audio — human recordings from native and fluent speakers (Tatoeba, CC-BY), not robotic text-to-speech.
• Useful phrases first — practical language you can actually say, chosen for enthusiasts rather than exam-takers.
• Study, then prove it — flip through a lesson, then test yourself with recognition and recall quizzes.
• Optional transliteration — see Latin-letter spellings while you get comfortable with Cyrillic, then switch it off.
• Gamified progress — earn XP, keep a daily streak, and watch each unit's mastery climb.
• Smart review — spaced-repetition scheduling brings phrases back just before you'd forget them.
• Fully offline — everything is bundled in the app. No account, no network, no ads, no tracking.

Whether you're planning a trip, reconnecting with family roots, or just love the sound of Russian, SLOVO makes daily practice quick and satisfying.

Audio clips are from the Tatoeba project (tatoeba.org) and licensed CC-BY; full per-clip attribution is available in the app under YOU → About → View Full Credits.
